Puzzle Jar (formerly Route Duel) – Support

Help and contact

How to play

Puzzle Jar has seven puzzle games: Bolts & Jars, Sweet Stacks, Button Tin, Ribbon Box, Bead Swap, Quilt Pins and Route Duel. Pick a game on the Home screen, or play today's daily puzzles to keep your day streak. Each game shows how to play on its first level. Stuck? Use a hint, a take back or a rescue.

Route Duel players: this is the same app with a new name. Your Route Duel progress and purchases are kept.

Your progress

Your levels, daily streak, hints and purchases are saved on your device. "Restore purchases" in Settings brings purchases back on a new phone. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Settings

Tap the gear on the Home screen to turn sound, vibration and reduced motion on or off.
