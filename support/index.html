Help and contact

How to play

Puzzle Jar has 20 puzzle games in one app: Bolts & Jars, Sweet Stacks, Button Tin, Nut Sort, Bead Swap, Mosaic Tray, Toy Chest, Hop On, Pencil Case, Snug Skeins, Shelf Sort, Treat Drop, Honey Hive, Fruit Tumble, Balloon Bunch, Tide Pool, Garland Tangle, Bubble Picnic, Quilt Pins and Route Duel.

Tap Play on the Home screen to follow the level road (three levels of one game, then the next game), pick any game under Free play, or play today's daily puzzles to keep your day streak. Each game shows its rules on its first level, and again from the "?" button. Stuck? Use a hint, a take back or a rescue; the first three take backs on every level are free.

Every game, with tips for each one, is explained on our website: sultanofniyyat.github.io/puzzle-jar (see also the help page).

Route Duel players: this is the same app with a new name. Your Route Duel progress and purchases are kept, and Route Duel is still inside as one of the 20 games.

Your progress

Your levels, day streak, helpers and settings are saved on your phone only; there is no account or cloud save. Deleting the app also deletes your progress. On a new phone, "Restore purchases" in Settings brings back Remove ads and other one-time purchases; hints, take backs and rescues that were used or bought as packs are not restored.

Ads and purchases

Puzzle Jar is free. A full-screen ad can show between levels (never during one), after a short "Ad loading" notice. A one-time Remove ads purchase stops those ads in all 20 games and makes take backs unlimited; optional videos you choose to watch for a reward stay available. Ads and purchases need an internet connection; the games themselves play offline.

Settings

Tap the gear on the Home screen to turn sound, music, vibration and reduce motion on or off, buy Remove ads, restore purchases and, in Europe and the UK, change your privacy choices.
