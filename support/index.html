Route Duel – Support

Help and contact

How to play

Take turns with the rival. On your turn, tap a route whose path to the edge of the board is clear: it launches and scores its length in cells. Careful – your launch can free routes for your rival. A tap on a blocked route loses your turn. When the board is empty, whoever cleared the most cells wins.

Your progress

Your level, wins and losses are saved on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Settings

Tap the gear on the Home screen to turn sound, vibration and reduced motion on or off.
