Puzzle Jar (formerly Route Duel) for Android – Privacy Policy

Developer: Sultan of Niyyat · Effective date: 8 November 2026 (version 2.0)

In short: Puzzle Jar is a collection of seven puzzle games. From version 2.0 the Route Duel app on Google Play is called Puzzle Jar; it is the same app, and Route Duel is one of its games. We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and your progress is saved only on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID, and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. You can remove ads with a purchase.

What this policy covers

This policy describes the Android game Puzzle Jar, formerly Route Duel (package com.sultanofniyyat.arrowrush.game), published on Google Play by Sultan of Niyyat.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-in and no analytics, and we run no servers. We do not receive your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other information about you. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects some information on its own, as described under Advertising below.

Information stored on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores a small amount of game data on your phone only: your level in each game, your daily puzzles and day streak, how many hints, take backs and rescues you have, which purchases you own, a simple count of which first steps of the game you reached, and your settings (sound, music, vibration and reduce motion). We never receive this data. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game; items you bought for good through Google Play come back when you reinstall.

Internet use

You can play every game offline. The game goes online only to load ads and when you choose to buy something, which opens Google Play's purchase window (see Purchases).

Advertising

Puzzle Jar shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between levels (never during one), and video ads you can choose to watch for a reward, for example a free hint or a rescue when you are stuck. To show and measure ads, Google collects information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, app diagnostics and how you interact with the ads. Google may use it to show personalised ads, for fraud prevention and for analytics. Google handles this information under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised; you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices button in the game's Settings. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying Remove ads stops the ads between levels; the optional video ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Purchases

Puzzle Jar offers optional in-app purchases: Remove ads, packs of hints, take backs and rescues, and a one-time starter pack. They are handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy, and we never see your name, card or payment details. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can unlock it.

Children

Puzzle Jar is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and its effective date changed before that version of the game is released.
