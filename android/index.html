Route Duel for Android – Privacy Policy

Developer: Sultan of Niyyat · Effective date: 20 October 2026 (updated for version 1.4.0: ads and in-app purchases)

In short: Route Duel is a turn-based duel game. We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and your progress is saved only on your phone. From version 1.4.0 the game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID, and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. You can remove ads with a purchase.

What this policy covers

This policy describes the Android game Route Duel (package com.sultanofniyyat.arrowrush.game), published on Google Play by Sultan of Niyyat.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-in and no analytics, and we run no servers. We do not receive your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other information about you. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects some information on its own, as described under Advertising below.

Information stored on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores a small amount of game data on your phone only: your duel level, wins and losses, which helper passes you own (Hint, Take Back, Remove Ads), and your settings (sound, music, vibration, text size, contrast and motion). We never receive this data. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game; purchases made through Google Play come back when you reinstall.

Internet use

You can play every duel offline. The game goes online to load ads and when you choose to:

  • Buy something: Google Play's purchase window opens (see Purchases).
  • Rate the game: Google Play's own rating window opens. It is provided and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Share the game: your phone's share menu opens with a link to the game. You choose the app and the people you share with; we never see it.
  • Privacy policy: this page opens in your browser.

Advertising

Route Duel shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between levels, and a video ad you can choose to watch to unlock a helper for a few levels. No ads appear during a duel. To show and measure ads, Google collects information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. Google may use it to show personalised ads, for fraud prevention and for analytics. Google handles this information under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised; you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the game's Settings. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between levels; the optional video ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Purchases

Route Duel offers optional in-app purchases (helper passes and Remove Ads). They are handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy, and we never see your name, card or payment details. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can unlock it.

Children

Route Duel is a general audience game and is not directed at children. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and its effective date changed. Please check it from time to time.
