Route Duel for Android – Privacy Policy

Developer: Sultan of Niyyat · Effective date: 20 October 2026

In short: Route Duel is an offline, turn-based duel game. It does not collect, share or sell any personal information. Your progress is saved only on your phone. This version has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

What this policy covers

This policy describes the Android game Route Duel (package com.sultanofniyyat.arrowrush.game), published on Google Play by Sultan of Niyyat.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or the advertising ID, and we do not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores a small amount of game data on your phone only: your duel level, wins and losses, coins won, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, text size, contrast and motion). This data never leaves your phone. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

Internet use

You can play every duel offline. The game only goes online when you choose to:

  • Rate the game: Google Play's own rating window opens. It is provided and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Share the game: your phone's share menu opens with a link to the game. You choose the app and the people you share with; we never see it.
  • Privacy policy: this page opens in your browser.

Advertising

This version of Route Duel shows no ads and contains no advertising software.

Purchases

This version of Route Duel has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added in a later version, they will be handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Children

Route Duel is a general audience game. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and its effective date changed. Please check it from time to time.
