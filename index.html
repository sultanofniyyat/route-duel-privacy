Route Duel – Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Route Duel is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: Route Duel does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Route Duel has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level, your wins and losses, and your settings (sound, vibration and reduce motion) on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet and third parties

Route Duel works fully offline. It does not send information to us or to any third party, and it contains no third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children

Route Duel is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds a new feature, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that version of the game is released.
